Are launch sites in close proximity to railways?

Yes. Launch sites are located in close proximity to railways (< 2 km) to facilitate the transportation of heavy booster stages, bulky rocket hardware, and propellant components.

Are launch sites in close proximity to highways?

Yes. Launch sites are situated close to major highways (< 1–2 km) to provide efficient road transport for personnel, mission equipment, and general supply logistics.

Are launch sites in close proximity to coastline?

Yes. Launch sites are deliberately situated directly along the coastline (< 1 km) for safety; this ensures easterly orbital launch trajectories fly over the ocean, preventing hazardous debris or abort failures from impacting populated areas.

Do launch sites keep certain distance away from cities?

Yes. Launch sites maintain a substantial safety buffer from urban centers (typically 20–50+ km) to shield populated areas from extreme acoustic energy, launch shock waves, and potential explosion hazards.
